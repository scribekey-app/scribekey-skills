**Q:** What is osmosis?

**A:** The movement of water across a partially permeable membrane from a dilute solution to a more concentrated one.

**Q:** What happens to a plant cell in pure water?

**A:** It swells and becomes turgid.

**Q:** What keeps a plant's stem upright?

**A:** Its cells being turgid.

**Q:** What is plasmolysis?

**A:** A plant cell losing water in salty water.

**Q:** How does the concentration gradient affect the rate of osmosis?

**A:** A steeper gradient speeds osmosis up.

**Q:** How does temperature affect the rate of osmosis?

**A:** A higher temperature speeds osmosis up.

**Q:** How does surface area affect the rate of osmosis?

**A:** A larger surface area speeds osmosis up.
