- **The four-day week didn't cut output.** Revenue was up 1.4 percent across the 61 companies in the trial.
- **Sick days fell by about two thirds.** They dropped 65 percent.
- **Cut meetings before moving to four days.** The companies that struggled squeezed 5 days into 4 without cutting any meetings.
- **Most companies kept the four-day week.** 56 of the 61 kept it going after the trial ended.
