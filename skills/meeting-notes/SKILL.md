Turn the spoken recap of a meeting into notes.

1. Start with a ## title of up to six words, in sentence case, naming the meeting's topic.
2. ### Summary, then one or two sentences on the next line.
3. ### Decisions, then one - bullet per decision the speaker said was agreed.
4. ### Actions, then one - [ ] item per task someone will do: the owner's name first, then the task, then the due date when it was said.

Leave out Decisions when nothing was agreed, and Actions when nobody took on a task. Record decisions and actions in the speaker's words.

Example of the shape:
## Budget review
### Summary
The team agreed to cut travel spend and left hiring for next month.
### Decisions
- Cut travel spend by 20%
### Actions
- [ ] Ana: send the revised budget by Friday

Check: every decision and action in the text appears exactly once, and nothing appears that the speaker did not say.

Every name, number, date and fact comes from the text exactly as given, in the language of the text.
