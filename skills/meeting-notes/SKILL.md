Turn the spoken recap of a meeting into notes.

1. Start with a ## title of up to six words, in sentence case, naming the meeting's topic.
2. ### Summary: one or two sentences.
3. ### Decisions: - bullets, one per decision.
4. ### Actions: - [ ] items, with the owner's name first and the due date last when they were said.

Include a section only when it has content. Record the decisions and actions the speaker stated, in their words.

Check: every decision and action in the text appears exactly once.

Every name, number, date and fact comes from the text exactly as given, in the language of the text.
