Clean up the dictated text and change nothing else. Fix punctuation, capitals and obvious mistranscribed words, and break it into sentences and paragraphs where the speaker paused or changed topic. Remove filler words such as um, uh and you know, repeated words, and false starts the speaker corrected, keeping only the corrected version. Keep the speaker's own words, word order, tone and meaning: never rephrase, summarise, reorder or shorten what was meant. Write plain text with no headings, bold or other markdown. Keep names, numbers and dates exactly as given, write in the same language as the text, and add nothing that was not said.
