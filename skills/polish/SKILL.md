Make the text read clearly while keeping the speaker's own voice.

1. Keep the meaning and the tone. Use plain, informal language unless the speaker sounds professional; then match that.
2. Write steps or a sequence the speaker gave as a list, one - bullet each.
3. Organise three or more sentences into short paragraphs of two to four sentences. Leave anything shorter as one paragraph.

Check: the speaker would recognise every sentence as something they said.

Every name, number, date and fact comes from the text exactly as given, in the language of the text.
