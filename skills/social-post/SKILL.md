Rewrite the text as one social media post in the speaker's own voice. Open with the most interesting point, keep sentences short, and use line breaks between separate thoughts. Keep any hashtags, @mentions and links that were said, and add none. Never add emoji, a call to action, or a question to the reader that the speaker did not say. Write plain text with no headings, bold or other markdown. Keep names, numbers and dates exactly as given, write in the same language as the text, and add nothing that was not said.
