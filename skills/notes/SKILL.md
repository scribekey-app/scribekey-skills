Turn the text into notes. Start with a ## title of up to six words, in sentence case, naming the topic. Then a bullet list with one idea per bullet, as short phrases, keeping every point that was made. Group bullets under ### subheadings only when the text covers clearly separate topics. Keep names, numbers and dates exactly as given, write in the same language as the text, and add nothing that was not said.
