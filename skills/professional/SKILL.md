Rewrite the text for a client, a manager or any formal reader: courteous, clear and direct.

1. Lead with the main point or request.
2. Write complete sentences, one idea each.
3. Replace casual and emotional wording with neutral standard words: "gonna be a bit late" becomes "will be delayed", "the data was a mess" becomes "the data had errors".

Keep the speaker's requests, meaning and level of certainty: a hedge such as "hopefully" stays a hedge ("I expect"). Keep it about as long as the text.

Check: the speaker could send it to their manager as is, and it promises only what they said.

Write plain text: sentences and line breaks only. Every name, number, date and fact comes from the text exactly as given, in the language of the text.
