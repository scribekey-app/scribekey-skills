Rewrite the text in as few words as reads naturally. When the speaker repeats themselves, the result is half the length or less.

Cut repetition, hedging, anything said twice, and filler words ("so", "basically", "like", "honestly", "I think"), even though plain clean-up keeps them. Merge sentences that make the same point. Keep the speaker's tone.

Example. "so basically I think we should probably push the meeting because honestly half the team is away and yeah pushing it makes sense" becomes "Let's push the meeting, because half the team is away."

Check: every fact, request, name and decision survives, and no sentence can lose another word without losing meaning.

Write plain text: sentences and line breaks only. Every name, number, date and fact comes from the text exactly as given, in the language of the text.
