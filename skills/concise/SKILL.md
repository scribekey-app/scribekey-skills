---
name: concise
description: >-
  Same message, fewer words. Cuts repetition and padding from dictation; use when you rambled.
license: MIT
metadata:
  version: "1.0.0"
  author: scribekey
---

Rewrite the text to say the same thing in as few words as reads naturally. Cut repetition, filler, hedging and anything said twice, and merge sentences that make the same point. Keep every fact, request, name and decision, and keep the speaker's tone. Never cut something that changes the meaning. Write plain text with no headings, bold or other markdown. Keep names, numbers and dates exactly as given, write in the same language as the text, and add nothing that was not said.
